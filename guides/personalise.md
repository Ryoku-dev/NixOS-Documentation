# Make Ryoku yours

Ryoku is designed to be customised from the desktop. You can change an insane amount without ever opening a configuration file or writing a single line of code.

## Wallpapers and colours

Open **Ryogami** and choose an image, apply it and the supported UI colours adapt. The automatic palette called `Matugen` takes colours from your wallpaper so panels, widgets and accents and applications match the chosen wallpaper.

## Bars and launchers

Use **Ryoku Hub** to find the settings for your bar and app launcher. The available options depend on which RyoStore products are installed.

A **bar** is the strip of controls and information on your desktop. An **app launcher** is the menu or screen used to open programs. They are separate things, even if a particular bar comes with its own launcher design.

## Ryostore

RyoStore is Ryoku's catalogue of visual extras, browse by category and read what an item changes before applying it. Some items add custom appearances, others need platform specific packages. Ryoku developers do not maintain the Ryostore as it is community led, any issues with a specific add-on should be given to the creator/ maintainer of that specific Ryostore item.

> **NixOS detail:** Content such as themes is often shared with Ryoku on Arch, but system packages are managed differently. Don't blindly follow Pacman or AUR instructions on NixOS as they will **not work**.

## Hyprland or Niri?

Both are **tiling window managers**. They decide where windows appear and how they move. Ryoku supports both, but not every layout setting has a matching option in both.

You don't have to learn both. Start with the one selected during installation and switch only when you have a reason.
